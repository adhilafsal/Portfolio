DOCSPOT

AI-Powered Healthcare Appointment & Hospital Management Platform

DocSpot streamlines hospital workflows by digitizing doctor appointment scheduling, patient queue management, and department availability. Designed to reduce patient wait times and eliminate administrative friction in clinical environments.

  • ✓ Appointment Scheduling: Interactive doctor booking calendar with flexible slot reservations.
  • ✓ Live Patient Queue: Real-time patient arrival monitoring and status tracking for hospital staff.
  • ✓ Doctor Availability Cards: Department-wise active roster with slot counts and offline states.
  • ✓ Consultation Booking Timeline: Visual schedule representation with interval and gap analysis.
